# Signal Atlas

## Product and audience

Wael Fezari's portfolio helps recruiters and technical clients evaluate full-stack and applied AI work, download a CV, and make contact. Project records, experience, social destinations, routes and contact payloads remain factual. Main surface: Experience; CV and contact: Operate.

## Three directions considered

- **Signal Atlas, chosen:** an engineering exhibition. Asymmetric type and orbit geometry connect interfaces, intelligence and integrations. Space Grotesk with IBM Plex Mono for measurement. Graphite `#111214`, chalk `#f4f5f7`, vermilion `#ff5c39`. Precise circular linework, square controls with 8px corners, scroll-controlled spatial transitions. Recognizable by its handwritten particle signature, spatial transformations, outlined display words and signal rails.
- **Code Workshop:** a tactile build notebook. Compact two-column annotations, instrument photography and sectional diagrams. Warm white `#faf8f2`, navy `#132338`, yellow `#f4b942`. Humanist sans, tabular annotations, hard rectangular panels, mechanical sliding reveals. Approachable for client work, less suited to the requested cinematic motion.
- **Motion Poster:** a typographic exhibition. Oversized stacked lettering, edge-to-edge demo cuts, restrained navigation and bold lateral wipes. White `#ffffff`, black `#101010`, pink `#e93875`. Condensed sans, sharp angles and kinetic letter sequences. Expressive, but less efficient for assessing technical breadth.

Signal Atlas makes the connected-systems specialty visible while giving actual applications and project descriptions room to lead. Motion intensity 8, composition variance 7, density 4. No speculative claims or fabricated proof.

## Motion thesis

Focal moment: an authored handwritten WF signature sampled from connected cubic strokes, with a clearer W, a looped F and a trailing flourish. The hero, header, footer, static fallback and favicon share the same signature curves. Its 6,111 shared particles carry the identity through the whole hero. Ambient curl and rotation give the signature depth; the pointer scatters nearby particles through spring response, then they return to their form. A portrait sits beside the name and role in the opening identity scene. The opening also carries orbiting local time, approximate IP location, local weather and IP, which fade as the journey begins. Context loading and unavailable values remain readable.

Startup: all nine advertisement articles begin hidden, transparent and excluded from the accessibility tree in the server-rendered page. The opening identity stays visible while renderer readiness starts false. The stage fades in once on mount over 850ms with cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1); the shared WF fallback crossfades to the canvas over the same duration when rendering becomes ready. The first scroll update adopts the actual target directly, including restored scroll positions, before later input uses interpolation. Reduced motion disables the opening fade and readiness transitions.

Continuity: native scrolling drives a sticky exhibition track (3000svh on desktop, 2600svh at the mobile breakpoint). The same particles morph through five factual personal stages: a gaming screen/controller, a code terminal, a neural brain, a connected team and a delivered application with client feedback. Camera movement, depth sorting and transition curl connect these shapes. Hero copy opacity, blur and a 12px vertical shift follow scroll progress. All five journey entries share one stage and crossfade with the particle shapes; their entrances are not keyed timed animations. Nine project particle sculptures populate the portal, while five journey particle sculptures orbit outside it; GetRatchet becomes the central focus as the journey enters the first advertisement. This leads into nine successive project advertisements. Stationary posters dissolve through a dither mask over local project progress 0.30–0.45; the shared particles sample actual artwork colors and leave rectangular poster positions. The outgoing contour holds over 0.49–0.58, morphs to the next project over 0.58–0.76, and the incoming contour holds over 0.76–0.86. The next poster emerges through its mask over 0.86–1.00.

The project advertisements use original generated raster artwork with cinematic three-dimensional materials and lighting. These are symbolic compositions inspired by the projects, not application screenshots or rigged three-dimensional subjects. Each frame puts a project title, one short phrase and an exploration CTA over full-bleed artwork, with softened image and frame edges. Desktop uses a landscape composition; mobile crops the artwork into a portrait frame with a bottom overlay. Hover or keyboard focus on the active advertisement starts a purpose-specific canvas action within a fixed composition. All nine actions use clean generated plates and isolated moving components. QueryOtter and PatchGoblin move body and working arm/tool sprites; GetRatchet turns its gear and carries cargo behind a foreground belt; Hooka Relay sends messages behind braces; Study Room turns a page about a fixed book-spine hinge; RAG Bench sends documents through lenses; RWeVibing turns records with fluid highlights and notes; Watchtower sweeps a soft beam; RecipeBuddy alternates spoon expressions and tosses separate tomato and basil components. The rebuilt actions were reviewed in paired current captures. The artwork camera stays fixed; these are authored raster component rigs and light actions, not a physical simulation. The canvas runs only for the active card while awakened and stops on pointer exit, blur, pause, reduced motion or a hidden tab. There are no next-project arrows or repeated scroll prompts in this advertisement sequence. Each CTA opens the matching detailed project through its exact project ID and URL hash. The personal lab below retains actual screenshots, demonstrations, technologies and public destinations.

The final poster fades over internal journey progress 0.918–0.930. The particles gather into a sphere over 0.924–0.940, pulse, burst outward over 0.946–0.958 and reform into the signature over 0.960–0.980. A clear centered WF holds at scale 0.76 with nine orbiting project marks and a tenth outlined tile labelled “Your idea” or “Votre idée”. The nine marks combine four sourced application SVGs and five authored particle contours with normalized bounds in colored square tiles. Their orbit appears over 0.974–0.980. Continued scrolling focuses the tenth tile over 0.984–0.991 and expands it into an aperture over 0.989–0.996. The existing invitation reveals only over 0.993–0.999, with the WF and nine marks settling beside its copy. The tenth tile and localized caption are procedural CanvasTexture geometry using the computed project font and a 14px outline; its ambient pulse stops with pause or reduced motion and adds no raster asset. The portrait stays in the opening identity scene.

The track maps raw scroll progress 0–0.8 to internal journey progress 0–0.93 and raw 0.8–1 to internal 0.93–1, preserving project reading time while reserving the final 20% for the ending. Chapter controls use the inverse mapping to bypass the long track. The contact CTA extends the exhibition without a dashed placeholder card. Pause stops ambient and pointer motion while navigation remains available. Rendering stops offscreen and in hidden tabs. Reduced motion or an unsupported renderer collapses the hero track into a readable static identity with expandable full journey text; detailed projects remain accessible below. The fallback is the shared WF signature.

The detailed desktop project reel retains its curved path, depth, banking and gentle rotation; mobile retains a native swipe carousel. Scroll rendering follows input with a short interpolation tail. Work and experience form one factual sequence, grouping professional projects under the roles where they were built and retaining education. The separate Connect pieces section and lower visitor panel are removed; visit context belongs to the opening.

Professional illustrations describe actual work: team scheduling and Lucca sync, document retrieval and Azure OpenAI, and Microsoft SAML/Office alongside a separate Bubble operations application. They are labelled feature schematics. Flow lines animate only while visible. Reduced motion stops ornamental movement while retaining functional navigation. Individual demo recordings retain play/pause controls.

Feedback: 150ms press feedback, 220ms control changes, 600ms spatial settling, cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1). Page content is visible by default. No custom cursor, scroll hijacking, timer-driven slide advance or compulsory intro. Reduced motion presents the journey as an expandable readable sequence and stops autoplay demos until the visitor chooses to play them.
