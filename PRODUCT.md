Facts and assets come from the portfolio and the owner's project repositories. Three professional case studies, nine personal applications, three experience entries and two education records. Preserve public anchors, CV endpoint, locale endpoint, contact payload fields, delivery validation, recipient, social URLs and analytics integrations.
